This change implements personal design favorites; custom sections, their ordering/drag gestures, folders, Brand templates and exact original comparison remain required work.

Home and Projects cards now offer a keyboard-accessible thumbnail star and Star for you/Unstar in More. Starred shows active favorites with search, an empty state and All projects navigation. Desktop Projects includes Your starred links; narrow layouts expose Starred beside All projects and Trash. The editor's previously inert Projects entry now opens a searchable Designs/Starred panel with local previews, current-design indication, star/menu controls and explicit links to open designs. Search and filter survive switching panels. Opening a design navigates to its editor; importing its pages into the current design remains unfinished.

Favorite metadata lives inside each account's existing local IndexedDB library. It does not change artwork, modification dates or artwork history. Editor saves merge the current star into artwork snapshots, so Undo/Redo cannot revert a favorite changed after an older history entry. Trash hides favorites, restoration retains the star, and permanent deletion removes the design. Ordinary copies are initially unstarred; explicit backup recovery retains favorites. Guest handoff preserves them with its existing independent-copy protocol. This remains local to the browser/account and does not introduce remote storage or a new service.

Backup/record version 12 retains favorites and rejects downgraded files. Libraries without favorite metadata continue writing their prior supported format. Validation rejects non-boolean stars. Backup imports retain independent IDs, page links, notes and original artwork. Existing atomic-revision protection, failed-write memory retention, backup and retry apply to favorite changes. Another tab detects a changed library and requires reload; automatic cross-tab merging remains unfinished. Clearing browser storage also clears local favorites.
